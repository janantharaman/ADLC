System landscape diagrams provide a comprehensive view of an organization's technology ecosystem, illustrating the relationships between Salesforce products, external systems, and integration points. These visual representations help architects understand the complete system topology, identify dependencies, and plan strategic implementations. They serve as essential documentation for stakeholders to visualize how different components interact within a Customer 360 architecture and support business objectives.
