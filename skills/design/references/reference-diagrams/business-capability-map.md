## Description

A template capability map for depicting business drivers and capabilities with high-level product positioning.
Business drivers are the high-level strategic goals or external pressures that push an organization to change how it operates.
Example business drivers include:

- Revenue Growth
- Operational Cost Reduction
- Customer Retention
