## Description

A diagram depicting Salesforce Data 360 in an end-to-end solution for marketing activations. Turn your data into immediate action with Salesforce Data 360. By leveraging out of the box connectors and zero copy interoperability Data Lake house Platforms, you eliminate complex data movement and get straight to execution. This seamless connection acts as a dynamic Unification and Activation Layer across all federated platforms—triggering the activations, intelligent, real-time actions required to become a fully Agentic Enterprise.
