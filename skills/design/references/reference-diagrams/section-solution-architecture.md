Solution architecture diagrams illustrate the technical design and implementation details of specific solutions, showing how components, services, and integrations work together to address business requirements. These diagrams provide architects and developers with a detailed view of system interactions, data flows, and architectural patterns. They serve as blueprints for implementation, helping teams understand component relationships, integration points, and technical dependencies within a solution.
