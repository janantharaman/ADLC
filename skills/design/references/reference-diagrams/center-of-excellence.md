## Description

A diagram showing how a Center of Excellence (CoE) bridges executive stakeholders and IT.
It highlights how functional and technical leadership capabilities are central to a CoE.

Centers of Excellence provide expertise and guidance across the enterprise.
They provide value with activities such as:

- Establishing best practices for the implementation of the solutions
- Enforce adherence to corporate standards and industry standard practices
- As product experts, educate and train the project teams in the products
- Solve hard problems that arise during implementation
