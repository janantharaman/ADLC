# Agentforce Public Sector (Public Sector Solutions) — Overview

> Source: salesforce.com/solutions/industries/government/public-sector-solutions/ (fetched 2026-05-19)

## What It Is

Agentforce Public Sector (formerly Public Sector Solutions / PSS) is Salesforce's industry cloud for government agencies — federal, state, local, and international. It combines a purpose-built data model, pre-built OmniStudio experiences, and Agentforce AI agents to digitise constituent services and back-office operations.

Built on the core Salesforce platform (Sales Cloud + Service Cloud infrastructure) with an industry-specific metadata layer. OmniStudio (OmniScripts, FlexCards, DataRaptors) powers the portal and guided workflow experiences.

---

## Core Modules

| Module | What It Does |
|---|---|
| **Benefit Management** | Automate benefit programs — eligibility assessment, enrollment, case management, payments |
| **Grantmaking** | Digital self-service grant applications, pre-built program templates, funding distribution |
| **License, Permit & Inspection** | Digitise intake, approval workflows, fee collection, inspection scheduling and tracking |
| **Investigative Case Management** | Guided workflows, secure collaboration, evidence management for complex investigations |
| **Workforce Management** | HR operations — recruitment, onboarding, workforce planning for public sector employees |

---

## Agentforce AI Capabilities

Out-of-the-box AI agents configured for public sector use cases:

- **IT Support Agent** — 24/7 ticket creation, incident management, self-service resolution
- **Benefits Eligibility Agent** — pre-screening constituents before human review
- **Complaints Agent** — NLP-based complaint intake, case creation, routing
- **Compliance Agent** — violation identification, enforcement recommendation automation
- **HR Screening Agent** — resume screening, candidate assessment, scheduling
- **Field Scheduling Agent** — inspection and service scheduling optimisation

---

## Digital Service Features

- **Omni-channel case routing** — capacity-based and case-type-based routing across phone, web, email, chat
- **Constituent Snapshot** — unified 360 view combining case history, benefits, licenses, and interactions
- **Self-service Help Center** — public-facing portal with web form submission (built on Experience Cloud)
- **Dynamic Forms** — low-code adaptive forms with conditional logic for eligibility intake and permit applications
- **Complaints Processing** — NLP-driven complaint categorisation and auto-routing
